Bio

Hello! name is Sheldon. Ever since I was young, I've always been a huge fan of both graphic and illustrative work! This is probably thanks to my older sister being an artist herself, as well as my very nerdy interests growing up such as anime and video games. Nonetheless it had inspired me to learn both mediums. It is a dream of mine to one day work in a field related to either of these!

When I am not working on a new project or my schoolwork, you can assume I am either gaming, listening to music, or watching a show as I still very much have interest in nerdy things.

Hopefully throughout your visit of this website you can see that I am passionate about this! I hope it also shows that I am eager to keep learning and improving my skills. If it has inspired you to contact me for work opportunities, than I sincerely thank you so much for considering me!

Software and Hardware

For all of my digital illustrations, I draw using Clip Studio Paint on a Samsung Galaxy Tab S10.

For all of my graphic design, I use the Adobe suite on a home computer. This includes programs like Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign.
